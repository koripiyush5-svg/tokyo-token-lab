Dimensionality Reduction & Vector Space: Core Concepts

Essential foundation for understanding how AI models bridge text tokens and vector coordinates.

1. What is a sentence embedding?

A dense numerical vector that encapsulates the semantic meaning of an entire sentence. Unlike tokenization (which splits text into discrete IDs), an embedding translates concepts into continuous geometric coordinates.

2. What is a vector?

An ordered list of numbers (e.g., $[0.24, -0.81, 0.45, ...]$) representing a point or directional arrow in a high-dimensional space. In modern LLMs, embedding vectors often have 768 to 4096 dimensions.

3. Dimensionality Reduction

Humans can only perceive 3 spatial dimensions. Dimensionality reduction compresses 64-D or 768-D vectors into 3D (PC1, PC2, PC3) while attempting to preserve as much variance and semantic distance as possible.

4. What is PCA?

Principal Component Analysis computes the eigenvectors of the dataset's covariance matrix. It rotates the coordinate axes to point along the directions of maximum statistical spread (variance).

5. What do PC1, PC2, PC3 represent?

PC1 is the linear combination of original dimensions that captures the greatest variance. PC2 is the second most informative orthogonal axis, and PC3 captures the third. Together they form our 3D view.

6. Why do similar sentences cluster?

Sentences sharing semantic topics (such as AI, computers, or Tokyo) activate similar subword features. In vector space, their angle is small, yielding high Cosine Similarity and proximity in the 3D plot.

SECTION 06 // KNOWLEDGE VAULT

Learning Corner

Master the theoretical underpinnings of Natural Language Processing, tokenizers, vocabulary mechanics, and vector spaces.

MODULE 01

What is tokenization?

The process of segmenting continuous strings of human text into discrete atomic chunks called tokens. Computers cannot natively understand grammar or meaning; tokenization translates characters into integers that neural networks can process.

MODULE 02

Why do LLMs use tokens?

Word-level tokenization would require a dictionary of millions of words and would fail on typos or new slang. Character-level tokenization makes sequences too long. Subword tokens provide the optimal balance between dictionary size and sequence length.

MODULE 03

What are token IDs?

Every unique token in an LLM’s vocabulary has an assigned index (integer). For example, in GPT-4’s tokenizer, "Tokyo" might map to integer 21542. The model embeds these integer IDs into matrices of float32 weights.

MODULE 04

What is Byte Pair Encoding?

Originally a data compression algorithm (Gage, 1994), BPE was adapted for NLP by Sennrich et al. (2015). It starts with individual characters and iteratively merges the most frequent adjacent bigram pairs in the training corpus.

MODULE 05

What is vocabulary size?

The total count of distinct subwords an LLM knows. GPT-2 had 50,257 tokens, LLaMA-3 uses 128,256 tokens, and GPT-4 (cl100k_base) uses 100,277 tokens. Larger vocabularies compress text more efficiently but require larger embedding matrices.

MODULE 06

How are emojis tokenized?

Emojis are multi-byte UTF-8 sequences. In byte-level BPE, an emoji like ⚡ (3 bytes: 0xE2 0x9A 0xA1) or 🇯🇵 (flag combination) is either recognized as a dedicated token or decomposed into its fundamental raw bytes without throwing an error!

MODULE 07

What are context windows?

The maximum number of tokens an LLM can attend to in a single forward pass. Because attention memory scales with sequence length squared ($\mathcal{O}(N^2)$), efficient tokenization that compresses text into fewer tokens directly maximizes effective reasoning window!

MODULE 08

What are sentence embeddings?

While tokens represent local linguistic fragments, sentence embeddings pool the entire sequence into a single fixed-length vector in semantic space. They power semantic search, vector databases, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

MODULE 09

How does PCA work?

Principal Component Analysis performs an orthogonal linear transformation to project correlated high-dimensional vectors onto uncorrelated axes of maximum variance, preserving global semantic cluster structures in 3D.
